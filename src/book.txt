GETTING STARTED

Welcome to your CrowPanel Reader. This short guide lives inside the firmware, so there is always something to read, even without an SD card.

Rotate the dial down to turn the page, and up to go back. Every page turn is a full refresh, so the text stays crisp.

THE CONTROLS

The dial turns pages. Pressing the dial opens the contents of the book you are reading: rotate to pick a chapter, press to jump to it, or press EXIT to return to your page.

MENU takes you to the library. EXIT takes you to the home screen. Your place in every book is saved automatically.

ADDING BOOKS

Copy .epub or .txt files to the SD card, either in the root folder or in a folder called books. Press MENU on the library screen to look for new files.

You can also add books without removing the card. Choose "Add books over Wi-Fi" at the top of the library. Scan the first QR code with your phone to join the reader's Wi-Fi network, then scan the second to open the upload page. Pick your books, upload them, and press EXIT on the reader when you are done.

THE HOME SCREEN

Put a JPEG named bg.jpg in the root of the SD card and it becomes your home screen. Any size works: the picture is scaled to fill the screen and dithered for the e-paper panel.

HAPPY READING

That is everything. Press MENU to open the library and pick your first book.
